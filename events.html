Annual Sports Meet

Date: Nov 15, 2026

Time: 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM

Location: Student Center

An annual sports meet is a vibrant school or college event that celebrates physical fitness, teamwork, and athletic talent through track-and-field competitions, fun games, and a grand award ceremony.

Key Elements of an Annual Sports Meet

• Opening Ceremony: Includes the flag hoisting, a disciplined march past by student houses, an oath-taking for fair play, and the lighting of the sports torch.
• Athletic & Track Events: Features sprints (like 100m and 400m races), relay races, long jumps, high jumps, and throws like the shot put.
• Team & Fun Games: Involves group activities such as tug-of-war, sack races, or team sports like volleyball and football.
• Closing & Prize Distribution: Concludes with teachers and chief guests awarding medals, certificates, and trophies to individual winners and the overall champion house.
